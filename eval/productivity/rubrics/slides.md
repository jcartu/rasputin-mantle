# Slide deck quality rubric

Score 0-10 for the usefulness and craft of the produced slide deck.

- 9-10: Clear story arc, complete coverage of the requested sections, concise slide titles, readable bullets, coherent ordering, and visual hierarchy that makes the deck usable by an executive audience without extra explanation. Charts or visuals are meaningful when requested.
- 7-8: Covers the requested content with generally clear titles and bullets, but has minor gaps, generic phrasing, or limited visual polish.
- 5-6: Structurally present but thin, repetitive, poorly prioritized, or only partially aligned with the task prompt.
- 3-4: Missing important content, confusing flow, overly sparse or cluttered slides, or visuals that do not support the message.
- 0-2: Not a usable slide deck for the prompt.

Do not give high scores for placeholder text, boilerplate, or slides that merely echo section names without substantive content.
