# Spreadsheet quality rubric

Score 0-10 for the usefulness and correctness of the produced workbook.

- 9-10: Workbook is organized into appropriate sheets, headers are clear, data types are sensible, formulas are used where calculations are required rather than hard-coded results, charts are included and readable when requested, and the workbook could be used directly by a business user.
- 7-8: Mostly correct structure and useful content with minor formatting, formula, or chart limitations.
- 5-6: Basic workbook exists but is thin, has weak formatting, limited formulas, hard-coded calculations, or incomplete chart/table treatment.
- 3-4: Significant omissions, confusing layout, incorrect or missing formulas, or data cleaning/modeling only partly completed.
- 0-2: Not a usable spreadsheet for the prompt.

Reward durable spreadsheet behavior: formulas, clear labels, separate assumptions/outputs when appropriate, and charts that communicate the data. Penalize static values pretending to be models.
